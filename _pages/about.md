Hao Ma received his B.Eng. degree from the School of Computer Science and Technology, Shandong University, Qingdao, China, in 2025. He is currently a Ph.D. student at Hong Kong Baptist University. His research interests include trustworthy large language models, social computing, and robust federated learning.

Outside of his academic life, he is interested in sports, especially badminton and running. He has run several half marathons. He grew up in Wulian, a beautiful mountain county in eastern China.
